Pulse 2.3.1 adds custom cursor images.

- Choose PNG, JPG or BMP images directly in Cursors & sound.
- Preview transparency, adjust size and choose a centered or top-left click point.
- Images keep their proportions and convert automatically to the Roblox cursor format.
- Existing backup, restore and per-game pack controls remain available.

Choose image, select size, close Roblox, then Apply cursor. Transparent PNGs give the cleanest result.
